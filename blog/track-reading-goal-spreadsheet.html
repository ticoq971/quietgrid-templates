How to track a reading goal in a spreadsheet (formulas included)

A reading log in a spreadsheet is private, flexible and yours forever. Here are the formulas to turn a simple list of books into real reading stats.

1. The columns you need

ColumnExample
A: TitleThe Lantern Atlas
B: Pages512
C: StatusFinished / Reading / DNF
D: Started2026-10-03
E: Finished2026-10-20
F: Rating4.5

Use a dropdown (Data → Data validation) for Status, so that "finished" and "Finished " (with a trailing space) don't get counted as different values.

2. Books finished this year

=COUNTIFS(C:C,"Finished", E:E,">="&DATE(2026,1,1), E:E,"<="&DATE(2026,12,31))

Counting by finish date means a book started in December and finished in January counts toward the new year, which is how most reading challenges work.

3. Pages read this year

=SUMIFS(B:B, C:C,"Finished", E:E,">="&DATE(2026,1,1), E:E,"<="&DATE(2026,12,31))

4. Am I on pace?

If you read evenly all year, the number of books you "should" have finished by today is:

=Goal * (TODAY() - DATE(2026,1,1) + 1) / 365

Example: with a goal of 30 books, on 10 October (day 283 of the year): 30 × 283 ÷ 365 = 23.3 books. If you've finished 23, you're 0.3 behind, which is basically on track.

Books per month needed to finish on time:

=MAX(0, Goal - Finished) / (13 - MONTH(TODAY()))

(this counts the current month as one of the months left).

5. Speed and ratings

Days to read:   =E2 - D2 + 1
Pages per day:  =ROUND(B2 / (E2 - D2 + 1), 0)
Average rating: =AVERAGEIFS(F:F, C:C,"Finished", E:E,">="&DATE(2026,1,1))

6. Show stars

=REPT("★", INT(F2)) & IF(F2-INT(F2)>=0.5, "½", "") & REPT("☆", 5 - ROUNDUP(F2, 0))

A 4.5 rating shows as ★★★★½.

7. A progress bar without charts

=REPT("█", ROUND(Finished/Goal*20, 0)) & REPT("░", 20 - ROUND(Finished/Goal*20, 0))

Wrap the parts in MIN/MAX if you might go over 100% of your goal. That's a nice problem to have.
